Stop re-typing your own orders

Every Shopify order that gets keyed into Odoo by hand is a chance to get the quantity, the tax or the customer wrong. Every stock figure that lags behind is an oversell waiting to happen. This connector keeps both systems telling the same story, continuously, without anyone touching a spreadsheet.

What syncs

Orders, both ways

New Shopify orders arrive in Odoo as sales orders in real time, line items, taxes and discounts included. Fulfillment status and tracking numbers push straight back to the store.

Products & inventory

Two-way product and variant sync. Inventory levels flow from Odoo to Shopify in real time, multi-location included, so you never oversell.

Customers

Customer records and addresses stay aligned in both systems, matched and deduplicated automatically.

Invoices & payments

Invoices and payments are created in Odoo automatically, refunds included, mapped to the correct journals and accounts.

Bulk historical import

Bring your history with you: import past orders, products and customers from Shopify into Odoo in one pass.

Abandoned carts → CRM

Abandoned checkouts are sent to Odoo CRM as sales leads, so your team can follow up instead of losing the sale.
